What it does

Three tools. Every league.

Managing one Sleeper league is easy. Managing ten is a chore. QuickManager turns hours of repetitive, league-by-league clicking into a few clicks that run everywhere you play.

01 — OPTIMIZER

Mass Lineup Optimizer

Scan every league at once and see exactly how many points you're leaving on the bench. Pick a week, review the swaps, apply optimal lineups everywhere with one click. Bye weeks and projections are handled automatically.

02 — WAIVERS

Waiver Wire Tools

Target any player and instantly see which leagues they're available in. Set FAAB bids and drops, then submit across leagues together. The Free Agent Radar surfaces the best unowned players by position, across your whole portfolio.

03 — TRADES

Trade Factory

Define a trade and scan every league for valid partners. Review every pending offer across all your leagues in one place — accept, decline, or withdraw without hunting league by league.

Setup

Two minutes, then it runs itself.

QuickManager connects to your existing Sleeper session — there's no account to create and no password to give us.

Desktop — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave

Install the extension

Add QuickManager from the Chrome Web Store (Chrome, Edge, Brave) or Firefox Add-ons (Firefox).

Log into Sleeper

Open sleeper.com in any tab and sign in as usual.

Open QuickManager

Click the toolbar icon. It connects automatically and pulls in your leagues.

iPhone & iPad — via Orion

On iOS, QuickManager runs inside the Orion browser (free, by Kagi) — the only iOS browser that supports Chrome/Firefox-style extensions. Not Safari.

Install Orion, then QuickManager inside it

Orion supports Chrome and Firefox-style extensions natively.

Open sleeper.com in Orion and log in

Then turn on Request Desktop Website from the page-settings menu — this is what makes Sleeper generate your login token.

Let the page fully reload

A floating button appears on the Sleeper page and turns blue once your token is captured.

Stuck on mobile? Tap the floating "Copy Token" button on the Sleeper page, then paste it into QuickManager's "Paste Bearer Token" box. This works even if auto-connect doesn't.
FAQ

Good questions.

Is my Sleeper login safe?

Yes. Your login token is read locally and stored only in your browser — it never leaves your device, and QuickManager has no server that could see it.

Why isn't my lineup change showing up?

Click "Refresh Rosters," then re-run the optimizer. Lineups are set per week, so double-check the Target Week matches the week you edited.

Do you support IDP leagues?

The engine is IDP-ready and will turn on automatically for defensive players once Sleeper publishes IDP projections — it currently doesn't, so rankings for those positions aren't available yet.

Is this affiliated with Sleeper?

No. QuickManager is an independent tool built to work with your existing Sleeper account. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sleeper.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open QuickManager and use the account management link next to your Pro badge — payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe, and you're never charged without a clear renewal notice beforehand.

Privacy Policy

What we do (and don't) collect.

Last updated: July 2026

QuickManager ("the extension") is a browser extension that helps you manage your Sleeper fantasy football leagues.

Data we access

  • Your Sleeper authentication token — read from your browser so the extension can act within your own logged-in Sleeper session. It is stored only on your device and never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Your Sleeper league, roster, and player data — read directly from Sleeper's servers by your browser, used only to display and act on your leagues. It is not collected or stored off-device.
  • Preferences and a cached player list — stored locally on your device so the extension runs quickly.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate a server that receives your data. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information, browsing history, or Sleeper credentials.

Payments

Optional Pro upgrades are processed by ExtensionPay using Stripe. Your email and payment details are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe under their own privacy policies. The extension only checks whether your account has an active pass.

Third parties

The extension communicates only with Sleeper (sleeper.com, api.sleeper.app) and, for payment status, ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com).

Affiliation

QuickManager is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sleeper.
