What it does

Four tools. Every league.

Managing one Sleeper league is easy. Managing ten is a chore. QuickManager turns hours of repetitive, league-by-league clicking into a few clicks that run everywhere you play.

01 — OPTIMIZER

Mass Lineup Optimizer

Scan every league at once and see exactly how many points you're leaving on the bench. Pick a week, review the swaps, apply optimal lineups everywhere with one click. Bye weeks and projections are handled automatically. Optionally prioritize your latest-kickoff players into FLEX spots, so you can still pivot as early games wrap up.

02 — WAIVERS

Waiver Wire & The Waiver Board

Add the players you want this week to one target list, and see where each is gettable across every league at once. Every claim it builds is fully editable — change the drop from a dropdown, or add your own fallback attempt if your first plan doesn't land. Manage claims you've already submitted right there too, with a true total exposure number against your budget. Free Agent Radar and Mass Drop round it out — surface the best unowned players, or cut a flier from every roster you added them to in one action.

03 — TRADES

Trade Factory

Define a trade and scan every league for valid partners. Review every pending offer across all your leagues in one place — accept, decline, counter, or withdraw without hunting league by league.

04 — MATCHUPS & POWER RANKINGS

Season Simulation, League by League

See this week's matchup and win probability everywhere at once, then simulate the rest of the season thousands of times for real playoff odds and a positional breakdown against the league. The Player Portfolio adds the gameday pass: every lineup you manage, in kickoff order, showing only the ones that still need a decision.

Lineup Optimizer

Applies changes you can actually trust

Each swap shows the real points gained or lost — never a fake number from two players simply changing slot labels. If nothing about the lineup's value actually changed, it says so plainly instead of inventing a trade that didn't happen.

  • Every OUT/IN row nets to real, verified points — no phantom swaps
  • "Slot changes made (no lineup change)" when the reorg has zero impact
  • Bulk-apply every league at once, or open any single one in the full editor first
Matchups & Power Rankings

Know where your week actually swings

See this week's matchup and win probability for every league at once, then run a full season simulation to find which leagues are still genuinely in play. A cross-league table ranks every roster by how much attention is actually worth spending on it right now.

  • Real-time win probability alongside the projected score, every league
  • Simulated playoff odds, sortable by week-swing, fragility, or weakest slot
  • Click any league for the full detail view — see below
Power Rankings — Detail View

Not just odds — the whole shape of your season

A fan chart of every simulated path shows exactly when your season's outcomes actually separate, with the real playoff cut line drawn in from the simulation itself. A positional table ranks your team against the rest of the league at every position, so you know precisely what to go fix.

  • Thousands of simulated seasons, condensed into percentile bands and a median path
  • Toggle between your optimal lineup and a depth/injury-adjusted model
  • Position-by-position rank against the whole league, not just a total score
Player Portfolio

Every player you own, across every league, at once

See your real cross-league exposure to any one player — who you're most riding on, and which of those leagues are actually still live. Age context flags players entering, past, or well beyond their typical prime, without ever predicting what they'll do next.

  • One list, every league — filterable by league type or position
  • Exposure percentage per player, with a live-league count next to it
  • Age-window flags (ascending, prime, past-prime, age risk) as context, never a forecast
Player Portfolio — Gameday

Every lineup you manage, in kickoff order

Sunday morning, a dozen teams, not much time. This finds the two or three lineups that actually need a click and stays quiet about the rest — an upgrade worth making, a starter who's been ruled out, an empty slot — then writes the change back to Sleeper without leaving the page.

  • Ordered by kickoff, because that's the deadline — anything already locked is marked, never offered
  • Every suggestion names both sides: who comes in, who goes out, and what the swap is worth
  • Tick the ones you agree with and apply them across every league at once
Waiver Wire & Free Agent Radar

Find the add, build the claim, in one screen

Free Agent Radar scans the whole player pool for standouts — filter by position or NFL team, include already-rostered players to scout trade targets, and add anyone straight to the Waiver Board. Manage every pending claim across every league without hunting league by league.

  • Position and team filters, plus an "include rostered" toggle for trade scouting
  • One-click add to the Waiver Board, single or in bulk
  • Mass Drop and pending-claim management for every league, in one place
Trade Factory

Deterministic Trade Impact

Before pulling the trigger on a trade, run a two-sided Monte Carlo simulation. QuickManager isolates exactly how a trade impacts both teams' playoff odds, and its optional depth-injury model reveals the hidden fragility in trading a star for scrubs.

  • Isolates the exact playoff odds impact using 2,000 parallel realities
  • Depth-injury model simulates random injuries across both sides of the trade
  • Works flawlessly for blockbusters involving both players and draft picks

Setup

Two minutes, then it runs itself.

QuickManager connects to your existing Sleeper session — there's no account to create and no password to give us.

Desktop — Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Firefox

Install the extension

Add QuickManager from the Chrome Web Store (Chrome, Edge, Brave) or Firefox Add-ons (desktop and Android).

Log into Sleeper

Open sleeper.com in any tab and sign in as usual.

Open QuickManager

Click the toolbar icon. It connects automatically and pulls in your leagues.

iPhone & iPad — via Orion

On iOS, QuickManager runs inside the Orion browser (free, by Kagi) — the only iOS browser that supports Chrome/Firefox-style extensions. Not Safari.

Install Orion, then QuickManager inside it

Orion supports Chrome and Firefox-style extensions natively.

Open sleeper.com in Orion and log in

Then turn on Request Desktop Website from the page-settings menu — this is what makes Sleeper generate your login token.

Let the page fully reload

A floating button appears on the Sleeper page and turns blue once your token is captured.

Android — via a Chrome-extension-capable browser

Most Android browsers don't support extensions, but a few Chromium-based ones do. Look for one that explicitly advertises Chrome extension or Chrome Web Store support.

Install a browser that supports Chrome extensions, then QuickManager inside it

Search your device's app store for a Chromium-based browser that lists Chrome extension support — most give you direct access to the Chrome Web Store to install QuickManager the same way you would on desktop.

Open sleeper.com in that browser and log in

Sign in as usual. If your token isn't captured automatically, check for a "Request Desktop Site" option in the browser's page menu — turning it on helps Sleeper generate the token correctly.

Open QuickManager from the toolbar

It should connect automatically once your token is captured.

Stuck on mobile? Tap the floating "Copy Token" button on the Sleeper page, then paste it into QuickManager's "Paste Bearer Token" box. This works even if auto-connect doesn't, on both iOS and Android.
FAQ

Good questions.

Is my Sleeper login safe?

Yes. Your login token is read locally and stored only in your browser — it never leaves your device, and QuickManager has no server that could see it.

Why isn't my lineup change showing up?

Click "Refresh Rosters," then re-run the optimizer. Lineups are set per week, so double-check the Target Week matches the week you edited.

Do you support IDP leagues?

Yes. Defensive players (DL, LB, DB) are ranked and optimized just like everyone else, using Sleeper's IDP projections and your league's own IDP scoring settings.

Is this affiliated with Sleeper?

No. QuickManager is an independent tool built to work with your existing Sleeper account. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sleeper.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open QuickManager and use the account management link next to your Pro badge — payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe, and you're never charged without a clear renewal notice beforehand.

Privacy Policy

What we do (and don't) collect.

Last updated: August 2026

QuickManager ("the extension") is a browser extension that helps you manage your Sleeper fantasy football leagues.

Data we access

  • Your Sleeper authentication token and account ID — read from your browser so the extension can act within your own logged-in Sleeper session, and so it can confirm the username you enter matches the account you're signed in as. Both are stored only on your device and never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Your Sleeper league, roster, and player data — read directly from Sleeper's servers by your browser, used only to display and act on your leagues. It is not collected or stored off-device.

    What "acting on your leagues" means

    When you use a feature that changes something, the extension sends that specific request to Sleeper using your own logged-in session — the same as if you'd clicked the button on Sleeper's own site or app. This includes setting lineups, submitting or editing waiver claims, proposing, countering, or modifying trades, moving players to or from IR or the taxi squad, and dropping players. Nothing happens automatically or in the background — every action is one you initiated.

    Sending a message on your behalf. When you propose or counter a trade, you can optionally check a box to also send the other manager a Sleeper direct message with the trade attached. This only happens if you check that box for that specific trade — it's never sent automatically, and the extension doesn't read, store, or otherwise access your Sleeper messages beyond delivering that one message.

  • Preferences and a cached player list — stored locally on your device so the extension runs quickly.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate a server that receives your data. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information, browsing history, or Sleeper credentials.

Payments

Optional Pro upgrades are processed by ExtensionPay using Stripe. Your email and payment details are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe under their own privacy policies. The extension only checks whether your account has an active pass.

Third parties

The extension communicates only with Sleeper (sleeper.com, api.sleeper.app) and, for payment status, ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com).

Affiliation

QuickManager is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sleeper.
