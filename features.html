Autosub NEW

Gameday tab, Management

Watches the leagues you pick in the 95 minutes before each kickoff, when inactives come out, and fixes what it finds. Part of Pro.

Replaces ruled out starters

When a starter is ruled out, it puts your best available bench player in his place, chosen from that league's bench with that league's scoring.

Fills empty slots

A starting slot with nobody in it gets the best eligible player on your bench.

Clears IR

Moves players who are out onto an open IR spot to free a bench spot, in leagues whose rules allow it.

Three modes

Log only records what it would do and changes nothing. Approval waits for you. Away Mode makes the changes and tells you afterwards.

Approval timer

In Approval mode, set how long it waits for you. If you have not answered in time, it goes ahead before kickoff.

An inbox of what happened

Every change is listed with the time, the league and the reason, grouped so one injury across four leagues reads as one line. Keep, dismiss or pick a different player.

Tells you what it could not fix

If nobody left on your bench can play the slot, or everyone who could has already kicked off, it says so, and says why.

Knows when it was not running

Autosub runs while the QuickManager tab is open. If it was closed when a game kicked off, it tells you how long it was gone.

Hands off what it should not touch

It never moves a player whose game has started, never makes waiver claims, never pulls anyone off your taxi squad, and only drops a player you have marked as droppable.

Optional extras

Park questionable starters in a flex slot so they are easier to replace, or let it set your best lineup outright instead of only fixing injuries.

Running banner

A bar across the top of every tab reminds you autosub is on and which mode it is in.

Export the log

Download everything autosub did as a CSV or JSON file.

Scoreboard and standings NEW

Gameday tab, Scoreboard and Season tab, Standings

Follow every matchup live, and see where every league stands.

Live win probability

Your chance of winning from here in every matchup, based on the points already scored and what your starters still have left to play.

Player by player

Open a matchup to see each starter's live points, projection and stat line while his game is on.

Pop out window

On a computer, open the scoreboard in its own small window and keep it on a second screen. It remembers where you left it.

Keeps itself up to date

Refreshes every minute while you are watching, without jumping the page around.

League median games

Leagues that also play the league median each week show that second result too.

Standings for every league

Records and points for all of your leagues in one place, with playoff odds from your last Power Rankings run and when that run was.

Click through

Click a league name to open the league, or a team name to open that roster.

Lineups

Lineups tab

Set the best available starters in every league you manage, in one pass.

Mass lineup optimizer

Sets the best starters across every league you have opted in, using Sleeper's weekly projections and each league's exact scoring.

Scoring per league

Reads each league's scoring rules on their own, so a TE premium league and a standard league get different answers for the same player.

Injuries and locked games

A player ruled out counts as zero, and a player whose game has already started is never moved in or out.

Any week

Run it for this week or set lineups ahead of time for a future one.

Leave the taxi squad out

Keeps taxi players out of consideration so a stashed rookie never lands in your lineup. If you allow it, it asks before taking anyone off the taxi squad.

Late games in FLEX

Keeps your best lineup but puts your latest kickoffs in FLEX spots, so you can still make a change after the early games.

Only real changes

Moving a player between two equal slots is not shown as a swap. Every change nets out to points actually gained.

Roster editor

Open any team to move players between your starters, bench, IR and taxi squad without leaving the extension.

Gameday board

Gameday tab, Management

What needs fixing before kickoff, across every lineup, and nothing else.

Every lineup, in kickoff order

One list of every lineup you manage, showing only what still needs a decision: a starter who is out, a player on a bye, an empty slot, or an upgrade worth making.

Both sides of every move

Each suggestion names who comes in, who goes out, the slot, and what the change is worth in that league's own scoring.

Apply one, or all of them

Fix a single lineup, or tick the moves you agree with and apply them to every league at once. Skipping one never breaks the others.

Ignore a move

Wave away a suggestion and it moves to an ignored list. It comes back only if the case for it changes.

Countdowns and locks

Shows how long is left before each game. Once a player's game starts he is locked and never suggested.

Live injury status

Designations are read live from Sleeper, with the body part and practice report a click away, and the board says when it last checked.

What cannot be fixed

When a starter is out and nobody on your bench can take the slot, the board says so instead of leaving the row off the list.

Sort and filter

Kickoff order by default, or sort by points or urgency. Filter to one position or league type.

Season simulation

Season tab, Analysis

This week's matchup in every league, and a full season simulation to see which leagues are still in play.

Weekly matchups everywhere

Who you are up against and how the projected score compares, in every league at once.

Win probability

Shown next to the projected margin for every matchup, always as your own chance to win.

Full season simulation

Plays out every remaining week thousands of times for real playoff odds, not a single projected record.

Depth and injury model

An optional second simulation that accounts for injuries across your whole roster.

Where your attention pays off

Every league ranked by how much this week's result swings your season, sortable by playoff odds, week swing, fragility or weakest slot.

How the season gets there

A chart of every simulated season week by week, with the middle 50 and 80 percent of outcomes, the median path and the playoff cut line.

Positional production

Ranks what your best lineup scores at each position against the rest of the league, so you know what to go fix.

Positional breakdown

Click any position to see every manager in the league ranked at that slot, with the players carrying it listed beside them.

Real starting slots

The number of players listed for a slot comes from your league's settings, and SUPERFLEX gets its own row instead of being folded into FLEX.

Weakest slot and fragility

Flags your weakest starting slot against the league median, and how much of your playoff odds an injury could cost.

League median games

Leagues that also play the league median every week have those games counted in the simulation.

League View

Full standings for any league with records, points for and against, and a lucky or unlucky signal. Every team opens to its roster and draft picks.

Exact draft pick slots

Picks show their real slot, such as 1.05, from the actual draft order, for snake, linear and third round reversal drafts.

Save and share a run

Save a full run as one page you can reopen, print or send to someone. See an example.

Starters CSV and spreadsheet

Export one row per team, per slot and per player, as a CSV or as a spreadsheet with each league on its own sheet.

Spy Mode

Look at another manager's roster without being able to change it. Anything that would act for them is switched off while you look.

Player Portfolio

Portfolio tab

Where your season long exposure actually sits.

Cross league exposure

Every player you own, how many of your leagues he is in, and which of those leagues are still alive.

Age context, never a forecast

Flags players entering, in, past or well beyond their position's usual prime, as context for a decision and not a prediction.

Filter and sort

Narrow the list by league type or position, and sort by exposure.

Waivers and free agents

Waivers tab

Find who is available across every league, then claim them without going league by league.

Lineup Upgrades NEW

Checks every league for free agents who would actually start for you this week, names the slot they would take, and puts them on your Waiver Board to bid on.

The Waiver Board

Put the players you want on one list and see which of your leagues each one is available in. QuickManager builds a claim for each, and you choose the drop from a dropdown or add a fallback.

Build from a ranking

Drag your targets against your own bench, best to worst, and QuickManager works out which drop goes with which add and in what order to submit them.

Bids from a percentage

Set the most you would pay for a player as a share of your budget, and QuickManager fills in the dollar amount in every league, allowing for what is already committed.

Cap on total FAAB

Set a ceiling on what a whole board of claims could cost in one league, and get warned if your cap is below your own top bid.

Claims you already sent

Pending claims show on the Waiver Board. Edit the bid, change the drop, or cancel, with a true total of what is already pending plus what you are about to add.

The right claim for each league

Detects whether a league uses FAAB, rolling priority or reverse standings and submits the right kind of claim.

Reorder by dragging

Change the order of competing claims in a league by dragging, the same way you would in Sleeper.

Makes room first

Checks whether a claim would fail for a full roster and offers to open a spot before you submit.

Watches pending claims

If your roster changes after you submit, it flags any claim that would now fail and offers to fix it.

Free Agent Radar

The highest projected unowned players across your leagues, filterable by position or NFL team, with an option to include rostered players when you are scouting trades.

Trending adds

Who the wider Sleeper community is picking up right now.

Season average rankings

Rankings use a player's average projection across the season, so a good starter who is banged up for one week is not buried.

Mass Drop

Drop a player from every league you own him in, in one step.

Suggested drops

Flags anyone on your rosters who is no longer on an NFL roster at all.

Trades

Trades tab

Build an offer once, find everyone who can take it, and handle what is pending.

Trade impact simulator

Plays out the rest of the season before and after a trade for both teams, so you see exactly what the deal does to each side's playoff odds.

Depth and injury risk

Optionally adds random injuries and bye weeks to both rosters, to show how a two for one trade changes how fragile your team is.

Find partners across leagues

Describe the trade you want and it searches every league for managers who can make it work.

Bulk propose

Send the same offer to every valid partner at once.

Draft picks and FAAB

Include draft picks and waiver budget on either side, with picks from other teams labeled by their original owner.

Every pending trade in one place

Incoming and outgoing offers across every league, with accept, decline and withdraw in one click.

Counter or change an offer

Reshape a trade someone sent you, or change one you made, without starting over.

Send a message with it

Optionally attach a Sleeper direct message to a trade so the other manager actually sees it.

A summary before anything sends

Every offer stops at a list of exactly what moves each way. Cancel leaves everything as it was.

Propose from the simulator

Aim a simulated trade at one manager, pick from their roster, and send it without rebuilding it.

Roster health

My Leagues

Catch the roster problems that quietly cost you points or get your team flagged.

IR rules per league

Works out who can go on IR under each league's own settings, and only offers moves Sleeper will accept.

Taxi squad rules

Respects each league's experience limit, taxi deadline and rookie rules, including the ones Sleeper does not enforce itself.

Move everyone eligible to IR

Finds every player who qualifies for IR right now across all of your leagues and moves them in one pass, after showing you who.

Find and fix illegal rosters

Spots rosters breaking their league's rules, like a healthy player still on IR or too many players, and fixes what it can without cutting anyone.

Cuts only as a last resort

Uses open IR and taxi spots first. If a cut really is needed, it says so and leaves the choice to you.

Drop a player

Cut someone from any roster with a confirmation, or use Mass Drop to cut him everywhere.

Setup and account

Header

Connecting, choosing leagues, and staying up to date.

Connects on its own

Sign in to Sleeper as usual and the extension picks up your session and checks it is really yours. On iPhone and iPad there is a paste option as a fallback.

Choose which leagues count

Opt any league in or out, and every tool respects it. Pro adds filters by league type.

Team and manager, together

Everywhere the extension names a team or a manager, it names both, the same way.

Update notice NEW

When a newer version is ready, a bar at the top says so, and one click updates and reopens QuickManager.
