changelog.txt

Version 2.0.4LATEST

Sleeper autosubs, an Analyze button for open trades, and a waiver bid fix.

NEW
QuickManager now reads the autosubs you set in Sleeper and can set them for you. In Edit Lineup each starter has an Autosub picker that lists only the bench players your league allows, and a bench player who is someone's autosub is labeled. The Lineup Optimizer can set an autosub for each Questionable starter, and the Gameday board suggests an autosub, or a better one, for Questionable and Doubtful starters.

Your Sleeper autosubs are protected. The Lineup Optimizer and Autosub each have their own setting for what happens when a lineup change would remove one, and both start at never. Edit Lineup and the Gameday board show you which changes would remove one and ask first. Rearranging slots never removes one.

Optimize in Edit Lineup picks the best autosub for each Questionable starter, and will move him to a FLEX slot when that lets a much better player back him up.

Autosub leaves a ruled out starter to his Sleeper autosub unless you tell it to replace him, and it never replaces one whose autosub has already played, since only Sleeper can still swap that player in. Clear IR no longer moves a player who is part of an autosub pairing.

Open trades have an Analyze button that loads the trade into the Trade Simulator and runs it.

Edit Lineup shows how many open bench spots you have and each player's injury designation.

The trade impact analysis now shows where each lineup slot ranks in your league before and after the trade, not just the points.

FIXED
A pending waiver bid can now be raised above the claim listed ahead of it in one save. The only limit is your remaining FAAB.

Version 2.0.3

One fix on top of 2.0.2.

FIXED
The Lineup Editor kept showing "Move to Taxi" buttons and the Taxi Eligible tag after the season had started. Sleeper's taxi deadline setting is a menu choice (no deadline, end of preseason week 1, 2 or 3, or start of the regular season), and the extension was reading it as a week number. A league set to "Start of regular season" looked open until week 4. Both now disappear once your league's deadline has passed. Removing a player from taxi still works, and leagues with no deadline are unaffected.

Version 2.0.2

One fix on top of 2.0.1.

FIXED
The "Move All Eligible to IR" button counted players from the saved player list, which is refreshed once a day, so on the morning after a week ends it was still reading last week's injury designations. The button would offer moves in a dozen leagues and then find nothing when you clicked it, because those players had gone from Out to Questionable overnight. It now checks the players it is about to name before it shows a number.

Version 2.0.1

A fix release for the first weeks of the season, mostly about moving to the next week at the right time.

FIXED
A player left on your IR slot who is no longer IR eligible, because he came off the real NFL injury list or his designation cleared, makes Sleeper reject every lineup change in that league. QuickManager had stopped counting that as a problem, so the Fix Illegal Rosters button never appeared and applying a lineup just failed. It is flagged again, the button repairs it, and autosub names the leagues it cannot manage until you do.
QuickManager now moves to the next week overnight after Monday's game, when Sleeper updates standings. It used to wait until Sleeper's own app changed weeks, which comes later, so lineups, autosub, the waiver tools and Power Rankings were still working on the week that had just been played. The scoreboard still shows last week's final scores until Sleeper moves on.
Power Rankings could count the week that just ended twice, once in each team's record and again in the simulation. If you ran it in the days right after a week ended, run it again for corrected playoff odds.
A dashboard left open, including autosub in Away Mode and the scoreboard in its own window, now notices when a new week starts. Before, it stayed on the week it was opened on until you clicked back into it.
Refresh Rosters now picks up a new week as well.
On a phone, Standings squeezed the team names out of view. Names now stay readable and the rest of the table scrolls sideways.
The Free Agent Radar's Rest of season view was labelled as weeks 1 to 18. It now shows the weeks it actually averages, from this week on.

Version 2.0.0

QuickManager 2.0 is the biggest update so far. It adds autosub, a live scoreboard and standings, and a long list of fixes to how injuries and locked games are handled.

NEW: Autosub
Inactives come out about 90 minutes before kickoff. Autosub watches the leagues you pick during that window. When a starter is ruled out, it puts your best available bench player in his place, fills empty slots, and can move players who are out onto IR. Choose Log only to see what it would do, Approval to decide each change yourself, or Away Mode to let it act. Everything it does goes to an inbox, and it tells you when a slot could not be fixed. It never makes waiver claims and never moves anyone off your taxi squad. Autosub runs while QuickManager is open in your browser and is part of Pro.

NEW: Live scoreboard
Every matchup you are in, with live scores and your chance of winning from here. Open a matchup for each player's points, projection and stat line as his game goes. On a computer, pop the scoreboard out into its own window.

NEW: Standings
Standings for every league now sit on the Season tab next to Power Rankings, with playoff odds from your last run. Click a league to open it, or a team to open its roster.

NEW: Lineup Upgrades
A button on the Waivers tab checks every league for free agents who would start for you this week, names the slot they would take, and puts them on your Waiver Board to bid on.

NEW: Update notice
QuickManager now tells you when a newer version is ready and can reload onto it for you.

IMPROVED
Injury designations are read live from Sleeper instead of from a player list that refreshed once a day.
The lineup optimizer no longer starts a player who has been ruled out, and never touches a player whose game has already started.
Leagues that also play the league median each week now count those games in playoff odds.
On the Gameday board you can ignore a suggested move. It comes back if the case for it changes.
The Gameday board no longer goes blank while it refreshes.
Applying a lineup that would take a player off your taxi squad now asks first and names who is affected.

FIXED
Some legal rosters were reported as illegal, most often with a player on IR.
The Lineup Editor offered taxi squad players as starters, and the save failed.

Version 1.6.1

A small fix on top of 1.6.0.

FIXED
Best ball leagues no longer show up in the Gameday board's warnings. Sleeper sets a best ball lineup for you every week, so there are no starters to read, and every slot was being reported as empty. Best ball leagues are now skipped on that board, the same way the lineup optimizer already skips them, and the board says how many were left out.

Version 1.6.0

Ready for the season, with a round of work on the screens you use most on a Sunday.

NEW: Positional breakdown
Click any position in a league's power rankings to open the whole league at that one slot. Every manager is ranked by what that slot is projected to score, with the players carrying it listed beside them, and you can sort by any column. It counts the starting slots your league actually uses, so SUPERFLEX gets its own row instead of being folded into FLEX.

NEW: Trades from start to finish
Point the trade simulator at a specific manager. Their roster becomes a list you can pick from, their draft picks come into play, and you can send the offer without rebuilding it, with a direct message attached if you want one. Every trade the extension sends now stops at a summary of what is moving each way before it goes.

NEW: Take the numbers with you
Export your starters with one row per team, per starting slot and per player, as a CSV or as a spreadsheet with each league on its own sheet. Saved power rankings pages now include the positional breakdown too.

IMPROVED
Teams and managers are named together, in the same format, everywhere the extension mentions either.
FAAB can be part of a trade, and a trade offer you attach carries players, draft picks and waiver budget.
League filters no longer offer choices that would show nothing.

FIXED
Attached trade messages came back blank after reloading Sleeper.
Several columns in the power rankings CSV export were always empty.
A notice about cached results in Power Rankings was cut off and could not be reached.

Version 1.5.0

The season simulation release.

NEW: Matchups and Power Rankings
See this week's matchup and win probability in every league at once. Simulate the rest of the season thousands of times for real playoff odds, with a chart of how the season could play out, how your roster ranks at each position, and a table of which leagues most need your attention.

NEW: League View
Full standings for any league, with records, points for and against, and a lucky or unlucky signal. Every team opens to its roster and draft picks.

NEW: Player Portfolio
Every player you own across all of your leagues, how many of your teams he is on, and which of those leagues are still alive.

NEW: Spy Mode
Look at another manager's roster without being able to act on it. Anything that would change their team is switched off while you look.

IMPROVED
Draft picks show their exact slot, such as 1.05, based on each league's real draft order.
The Waiver Board has an optional cap on total FAAB spend.
Free Agent Radar can filter by NFL team, include rostered players, and page through results.
IDP players are placed in the right positions everywhere.
The lineup optimizer shows only real changes, so moving a player between two equal slots is no longer reported as a swap.

Version 1.4.6

Roster tools, trade counters and a lot of waiver fixes.

NEW
Manage IR and taxi squads from the roster editor.
Move every eligible player to IR across all of your leagues in one step.
Find rosters that break their league's rules and fix them.
Counter or change trade offers from Open Trades, and send the other manager a direct message with the trade attached.

IMPROVED
Before a waiver claim goes in, QuickManager checks whether your roster has room and offers to make it.
Claims are submitted the right way for FAAB leagues and for leagues that use waiver priority.
If you type the wrong Sleeper username, you can fix it without reinstalling.

FIXED
Upgrading or starting a trial did nothing on iPhone and iPad. There is now a link that works everywhere.
